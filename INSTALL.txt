KODEIT TODO — INSTALL
=====================

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 11 or later.


1. INSTALL
----------

  From the .dmg : double-click it, then drag "Kodeit Todo" onto the
                  Applications shortcut beside it. Then eject the disk image.

  From the zip  : double-click to unpack, then drag "Kodeit Todo.app"
                  into your Applications folder.

Run it from Applications — not from the disk image or your Downloads folder.


2. THE FIRST LAUNCH IS BLOCKED. THIS IS EXPECTED.
-------------------------------------------------

macOS will say:

   "Kodeit Todo" can't be opened because Apple cannot check it for
   malicious software.

This app is not signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate, so macOS
cannot identify who made it and blocks it by default. The download is fine.
To allow it:

   1. Click "Done" on the warning.
   2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   3. Scroll to "Security". It says: "Kodeit Todo" was blocked...
   4. Click "Open Anyway" and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
   5. Open the app again. Every launch after this one is normal.

On macOS 15 and later, right-clicking the app and choosing "Open" does NOT
work for this. Use System Settings as above.

Terminal alternative, if you prefer:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Kodeit Todo.app"


3. USING IT
-----------

The app is in two places at once: a normal window, and a checkmark icon in
the menu bar showing how many tasks are due today. Closing the window does
not quit the app — use the menu-bar icon's Quit.

Press Option+Space from ANY app for a quick-add box over whatever you are
doing. Type, press Enter, and you are back where you were. Esc cancels.

Type naturally:

   Call the bank tomorrow p1 #Admin @calls

That is a task "Call the bank", due tomorrow, priority 1, in the Admin
project, labelled "calls".  #Project picks or creates a project, @label adds
a label, p1-p4 sets priority, and dates like "today", "next monday",
"25 Dec" and "every weekday" all work.

Press ? inside the app for all keyboard shortcuts.


4. YOUR TASKS STAY ON YOUR MAC
------------------------------

Everything lives in one file:

   ~/Library/Application Support/Kodeit Todo/tasks.json

No account, no sync, no server — nothing leaves your machine. Copy that file
to back up or move your tasks. Deleting the app leaves it behind.


5. TROUBLE
----------

"The application is damaged and can't be opened."
   The download was incomplete, or a chat app or cloud drive altered the
   .app on the way. Get a fresh copy of the .dmg or .zip.

Option+Space does nothing.
   Another app owns that shortcut — Alfred, Raycast and similar launchers
   often do. Quit or rebind it. The menu-bar icon's "Quick Add" still works.

No reminder notifications.
   Allow them in System Settings > Notifications > Kodeit Todo.

Nothing happens at all, with no warning.
   You are probably on an Intel Mac. This build is Apple Silicon only.
